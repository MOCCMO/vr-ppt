Judy Hopps classroom model

Original model: J.C. Thornton, Judy Hopps V3.
Source: https://www.patreon.com/jcthornton/posts/3d-model-judy-22565107
Character: Judy Hopps / Zootopia © Disney.

Classroom adaptation: police outfit selection, native iris UV preservation,
web materials, optimized textures, facial control selection, standing pose,
live bone movement and speech-driven visemes. Original source files are
preserved outside the web delivery. This credit is not a new license grant.

Voice: Qwen3-TTS synthetic voice design, approved by the user through an
English opening sample; subsequent speech uses that generated reference.
It does not use a movie audio clip or the actor's recorded voice.
